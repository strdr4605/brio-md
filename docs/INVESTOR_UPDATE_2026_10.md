### 4. Main Focus, Highlights & Lowlights

**This month, we'll focus on:**
Converting remaining demo pipeline leads into paying academies, shipping the automated CSV student migration wizard, and rolling out WhatsApp automated payment reminders to reduce school debt collection overhead.

**Our biggest highlights were:**
* **Seamless Flagship Migration:** Successfully migrated our flagship partner (Vibe Academy) completely off their legacy PHP system (Educurat) to Brio.md with zero downtime and 100% data integrity.
* **Quantifiable Admin ROI:** A school director reported that our High-Density Attendance Journal and Debtor Tracking table saved their administrative staff over 6 hours of manual spreadsheet reconciliation every single week.
* **Pricing & Packaging Validation:** Successfully transitioned new school signups from an early €65 beta tier to our standard **€95/month** tier by bundling the Student Portal (`learn.brio.md`) with the Staff ERP (`in.brio.md`), while proving self-serve tutor demand at **€20/month**.

**Our biggest lowlights were:**
* **LinkedIn Outbound Ineffective Locally:** Outbound response rates on LinkedIn with local school directors were very low (<4%). Directors respond much better to direct phone calls, warm community referrals, and in-person walkthroughs in Chișinău.
* **Messy Legacy Onboarding Data:** Onboarding 2 new centers took nearly 10 days instead of 2 days due to corrupted, non-standard Excel spreadsheets. We are mitigating this immediately by engineering a strict client-side CSV validation and mapping tool.
